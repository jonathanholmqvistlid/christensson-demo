About

Since 1902

The company was established in 1902 as a supply store. In the early 1920s, when oil demand grew, the bunker barge Sjöbjörn was purchased — used to transport fuel to Styrsö and bunker ships in the Port of Gothenburg. We sold our last bunker barge in 2000.

Today we use a large network of suppliers for fast, efficient delivery. The company is run by the third and fourth generation Christensson.

Our goal is personal service — a flexible overall solution for all bunker purchases.
